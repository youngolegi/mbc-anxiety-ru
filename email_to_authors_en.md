**Subject:** Permission request: Russian cross-cultural adaptation of the OASIS

Dear Dr. Norman, Dr. Campbell-Sills, and Dr. Stein,

My name is Oleg [Surname], a psychologist (CBT-oriented) from Russia. I am planning
a non-commercial academic project to adapt the Overall Anxiety Severity and Impairment
Scale (OASIS) into Russian, following the ITC Guidelines for Translating and Adapting
Tests (2nd ed.).

The project includes forward and back translation, an expert committee, cognitive
interviews, and a psychometric evaluation (factor structure, reliability, convergent and
discriminant validity, measurement invariance across gender and age, cut-scores), with a
particular focus on sensitivity to change in routine therapy, as the scale is intended
for measurement-based care.

I would be grateful for:

1. Your permission to translate and adapt the OASIS into Russian for research and
   non-commercial clinical use;
2. Any information about existing Russian versions you may be aware of;
3. The recommended source version of the items, response options, and scoring
   instructions;
4. Your guidance on any conditions of use (attribution, sharing the adapted version
   with you, publication).

I will be happy to share the Russian version, the technical report, and the results with
you. If you are open to it, I would also welcome advice on testing cross-language
measurement invariance.

Thank you for your time and for developing such a useful measure.

Kind regards,
